# VAS V2 — Planned Parent Journey Validation

**Status:** PLANNED. No participant sessions or measured UX outcomes are claimed.

## Product question

Can a prospective parent move from “I am considering VAS” to a reasoned next step — understanding a programme, finding a suitable campus and requesting a visit — without losing context or being pushed into a generic enquiry too early?

## Research questions

1. Can parents explain the difference between the programme choices well enough to choose what to investigate next?
2. Can they find a plausible campus using the information they care about?
3. Do they understand the relationship between programme, campus and visit planning?
4. Does the visit planner preserve enough context to avoid repeated work?
5. Do parents understand that a submitted visit request is **not** an automatically confirmed appointment?
6. Where do they hesitate, backtrack or abandon the journey?

## Participant profile

Directional sample target: **5 participants** who are:
- currently considering a private, bilingual or international school for a child; or
- have made a comparable school-choice decision within the last 24 months.

Recruit by behavior/context, not by demographic stereotypes.

This is a small usability sample. It is not statistically representative and must not be used to claim conversion uplift.

## Stable scenario

> Bạn có con 8 tuổi và đang cân nhắc một trường song ngữ/quốc tế tại TP.HCM. Hãy tìm hiểu xem chương trình nào có vẻ phù hợp, tìm một cơ sở mà gia đình có thể cân nhắc, xem đủ thông tin để quyết định có muốn ghé thăm hay không, rồi gửi mong muốn tham quan.

Do not tell participants which route or CTA to use.

## Tasks

1. From Home, decide where you would start.
2. Compare programme options and identify one worth exploring.
3. Find a campus that fits the scenario.
4. Inspect enough campus information to decide whether to visit.
5. Start the visit-planning flow.
6. Verify whether previously selected context was carried forward.
7. Choose a preferred date/time and reach the request-complete state.
8. Explain what you believe will happen next.

## Directional measures

### Primary
- task completion without moderator rescue;
- correct programme interpretation;
- campus-finder completion;
- visit-request completion;
- correct understanding that the visit still requires confirmation;
- whether programme/campus context survives into the visit step.

### Secondary
- time to find programme comparison;
- time to identify a campus;
- navigation reversals / backtracks;
- wrong CTA selections;
- repeated data entry;
- self-reported decision confidence (1–5) after programme comparison and before visit submission;
- notable confusion language.

## Evidence capture

For every session record:
- task outcome;
- route taken;
- first hesitation;
- backtrack or dead end;
- misunderstood label;
- missing information;
- context that was unexpectedly lost;
- exact screen/element where the issue occurred;
- proposed design change linked to that observation.

Do not fabricate quotes. Do not convert a moderator interpretation into a participant finding without supporting notes.

## Before / after discipline

If the legacy/public VAS experience can be tested under the same task definition, record it as a separate baseline round.

If comparable baseline testing is not possible:
- label current-site observations as **heuristic / IA baseline**;
- do not write “improved X%”;
- do not imply that V2 is faster, clearer or higher-converting without evidence.

## Guardrails

- A shorter journey is not automatically better if parents miss programme or campus evidence.
- Do not optimize only for enquiry submission.
- Keep self-guided decision support available before personal-data collection.
- Preserve explicit uncertainty around campus visit availability.
- No fake real-time slots, application acceptance or enrolment outcomes.

## Iteration rule

Change the experience when there is:
- a repeated usability issue;
- one severe failure that blocks a critical decision;
- verified accessibility/runtime failure; or
- real analytics evidence tied to a defined product question.

Otherwise preserve the current VAS art direction and avoid redesign-for-redesign's-sake.

## Portfolio reporting format

After real sessions, the case study may use:

**Baseline / hypothesis → observed behavior → design decision → revised flow → retest result → limitation**

Until then use **Planned validation**, not **Outcome**.
